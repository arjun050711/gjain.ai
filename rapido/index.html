01First principles

Start from what is physically true.

Strip away the ad-tech language and Rapido holds four primitives no search engine, social feed, or quick-commerce app has. Everything on this page is built from these.

Truth 01

It knows the future

The commute is a habit. Rapido doesn't just know where you are — it knows where you'll physically be tomorrow at 9:10am, with high confidence. That enables anticipatory advertising: a genuinely new targeting primitive.

Truth 02

Destination is intent

A destination is a declaration. Airport = travel. Mall = shopping mode. Showroom = purchase. Rapido catches intent 15 minutes before physical arrival — the last influenceable moment before money is spent.

Truth 03

It can see the visit

~90% of Indian retail is offline, where digital ads rely on modelled panels. Rapido holds something rarer: observed, ride-level ground truth when the journey ends at the store — real-world evidence, not a model.

Truth 04

It controls the fare

Rapido's users are India's most price-sensitive commuters. Brand money can do here what it can't anywhere else: pay for the ride itself. Advertising that subsidises the fare grows the core business instead of taxing it.

03The signals underneath

The mode you pick is data. The modes you graduate through are more.

Search sees queries. Social sees scrolls. Rapido sees lives moving through the city — and three signals fall out of that which no other platform in India holds.

Signal 01 · per trip

The occasion signal

The same person choosing bike on Tuesday morning and cab on Friday night is telling you their spending mood, company and occasion — per trip. Serve the ₹99 offer on the commute and the credit-card ad on the Friday cab. Same human, priced by the moment.

Search and social can't see occasion. Rapido is the occasion.
Signal 02 · over months

The trajectory signal

The mode ladder is India's income ladder. A rider graduating bike → auto → cab is a real-time upward-mobility signal: first job, first raise, new household. A frequent bike user who starts taking cabs is the two-wheeler-to-car purchase funnel — visible in behaviour.

For fintech, auto OEMs, insurance and telecom, that's an audience product, not just reach.
Signal 03 · the graph

The multi-modal Journey Graph

One identity across bike, auto, cab — plus parcels, plus food. Single-mode players see a slice; Rapido sees the pattern: home, work, weekly rhythm, spending shape. The fullest movement-and-consumption picture of the mass Indian consumer that exists.

This is the moat version of "location data" — and it's asset-light.
04The measurement edge

A real-world signal most ad budgets never get.

~90% of Indian retail happens offline, where store-visit measurement is modelled from panels. Rapido can add something harder to fake: observed, ride-level evidence — for the journeys it carries — across all three modes. Not universal attribution; a differentiated signal.

Step 1

Ad shown

A QSR, showroom or store campaign served at booking, in the wait, or mid-ride.

Step 2

Ride taken

The rider books to — or stops at — the advertised location. Bike, auto or cab.

Step 3

Arrival observed

GPS ground truth records the journey ending at the location — observed, not modelled.

Step 4

Evidence sold back

Ride-level visit evidence enriches the campaign report — a signal panels can’t match.

Observed ground truth for offline outcomes — a sharper signal than the modelled panels advertisers settle for today.

06The second audience

The other nine million.

Captains are usually discussed as supply. First principles says they're also consumers — earners with disposable income, whose earnings Rapido itself settles. No other consumer platform in India holds an audience like this.

Fact 01

Verified income

Rapido pays its captains — so it knows earnings, frequency and growth, verified weekly. For lending, insurance and BFSI, that's targeting gold no bureau or panel can match. Consent-led, it's a category-defining audience product.

Fact 02

Part-time by design

Bike captains especially work a few hours a day around other work — dual-income households with real disposable income and aspirations to match. Low commitment also keeps the pool vast and growing.

Fact 03

Idle-time attention

Between pings, a captain is parked, phone in hand, waiting. That's genuine daily screen time inside the captain app — a video-capable moment that costs the ride experience nothing.

Fact 04

Endemic demand

Fuel & EV, two-wheeler brands, lending, insurance, income tools — categories that specifically want earners on vehicles. Demand only Rapido can serve at this precision, on both sides of its marketplace.

07The video layer

Video, only where the attention is real.

Video is the high-attention, high-ARPU end of advertising — CPMs run at multiples of static, and it's where brand budgets are moving. Rapido can build a genuine video business, because it has four honest video moments — and the discipline to sell only those.

Rider · the wait

The brand film

Captain on the way, rider watching the screen — a full-screen, sound-on video moment with nowhere else to go.

Rider · the auto

Short video mid-ride

Seated, phone out, 10–20 minutes — short-form video and interactive stories that ride along.

Rider · the cab

Captain · idle time

Parked and waiting — daily video minutes inside the captain app, reaching the verified-income audience.

And one refusal that builds trust: never video on a moving bike. Selling only real attention is what keeps the CPMs premium.
08Beyond ads — the stack

What a daily paying base in India is really worth.

In India, ARPU is built by stacking small monetizations on high frequency — the super-app math. The hardest asset is a daily paying relationship with payment rails and trust attached. Rapido already has it, 4M+ times a day, on both sides of the marketplace.

RidesLayer 1 · the base

The daily paying relationship. Payment rails, verified identity, trust — the foundation every other layer monetises.

AdvertisingLayer 2 · attention

Everything above — mode-aware inventory, the Journey Graph, video, retail media. The highest-margin layer.

CommerceLayer 3 · transactions

Ownly food, parcels, hyperlocal errands — and mobility-native commerce like metro, bus and event ticketing inside the app people already open daily.

Financial servicesLayer 4 · the big one

Riders: transit BNPL ("ride now, pay weekly"), a co-branded card, trip insurance that upsells to health and term. Captains: lending underwritten by earnings Rapido itself settles — an underwriting edge no bank has — plus vehicle finance and insurance.

MembershipLayer 5 · loyalty

A rider subscription — priority matching, capped surge, Ownly benefits — plus coalition coins earned on rides and redeemed across partner brands.

EV & insightsLayer 6 · the ecosystem

EV leasing and financing at fleet scale — Rapido is India's largest two-wheeler demand aggregator — and privacy-safe urban and retail insights (catchment, site selection) sold to enterprises.

10The build

Twelve months to the platform.

Ship the self-serve platform

A first-party Ads Manager — static and video, mode-aware placements, closed-loop measurement — so demand isn't gated by agencies. The buyers are marketers, not riders: enterprise sales + agency partnerships + self-serve, built for the advertiser.

Lead with measurement

Ride-level visit evidence as the wedge into QSR, retail and BFSI budgets — sold honestly as observed signal alongside standard metrics, not as universal attribution.

Build the Journey Graph

One identity across modes, turned into privacy-safe audiences: occasion, trajectory, pre-arrival intent — and, consent-led, the verified-income captain audience.

Build Ownly ads-first

Zero commission works only if merchant advertising becomes the revenue line. Promoted restaurants and dishes from day one — the highest-intent inventory on the platform.

Pilot the fare flywheel

Sponsored commutes, occasions and customers — one flagship brand per mode, measured end-to-end.

One team, one P&L

Product, data, sales and ops owned end-to-end and accountable for the number — an ad business, not a feature.
