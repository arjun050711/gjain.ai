Cross-Domain Signals

The same individual across retail, grocery, fashion, travel & payments — a 360° consumer graph no panel and no other retailer can build.
🧬
Powered by PulseID — the identity spine
The privacy-safe layer that resolves the same individual or household across every Group property — using deterministic signals (login, phone, payment) and probabilistic matching into one consented, anonymised graph. PulseID is the foundation the entire moat is built on.

Why this is unique

Legacy panels see a small survey sample, weeks late. Pulse sees the same real person shopping on Flipkart, ordering on Minutes & Supermart, dressing via Myntra, travelling via Cleartrip and paying via super.money — letting brands answer questions no panel can, from real behaviour rather than a projected sample.

How the clean room protects everyone 🔐

Your data and Group data are matched on hashed identifiers inside a secure environment. You only ever receive aggregated, anonymised results and hashed audience keys — never another party's raw customer data, and never individual-level records.
