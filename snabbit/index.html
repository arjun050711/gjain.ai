—Sample brand pitches
Target: Priya · Ananya · Vikram — the premium household

Your product. Their home. Every week.

The insight: Ariel spends crores reaching SEC A households on Instagram — where they're skipping the ad in 3 seconds. Snabbit's maid is in their laundry room every week, touching their washing machine, looking at their detergent shelf. No impression. A presence.

The activation

Snabbler-delivered Ariel pod sample during every laundry-related service. Photo-verified placement. The Snabbler demonstrates the product. Household receives a ₹50 cashback QR code redeemable on the next Ariel purchase. SEC A home, zero wastage, measured conversion.

Scale

50,000 homes · weekly touchpoint · 12 weeks. Not a digital campaign. A physical distribution activation with digital measurement. The same household that sees the Ariel pod is retargeted in-app for the full pack. Physical-to-digital conversion loop.

Measurement

Purchase conversion tracked via QR code scan + app coupon redemption. Snabbit provides verified placement photos, household category segment, and conversion rate. ROI calculated per sample placed — not per impression served.

"You've been trying to get into their laundry room for 20 years. We go in every Tuesday."
Target: Ananya — the new mother · highest willingness to pay

The home a new mother trusts is your best sales channel.

The insight: Ananya researches baby products for 2 hours before buying. She trusts exactly two sources: other mothers and the people inside her home. A Pampers sample placed by a Snabbit nanny carries more conversion weight than a full-page print ad.

The activation

Nanny-delivered baby care sample kit — Pampers, Mamaearth, Himalaya — placed during every nanny booking in households with a child under 2. Nanny is trained to explain the product. Household receives a first-purchase discount QR for the full product line.

New mother segment

Snabbit identifies new-parent households from nanny/babysitter booking patterns. A verified new-mother audience segment sold to Pampers, Enfamil, Mamaearth, Himalaya, and Chicco — the five brands fighting for wallet share in the first 12 months.

Why Snabbit wins

Mamaearth pays ₹600–1,200 CPA on Meta for a new-mother customer. Snabbit delivers a physically verified in-home sample placement with nanny endorsement for ₹80–150 per home. The conversion rate is not comparable.

"She filters every Instagram ad. She does not filter what her nanny tells her to try."
